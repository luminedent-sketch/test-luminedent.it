How It Works

An implant, explained simply

A dental implant replaces the root of a missing tooth with a titanium screw, onto which an artificial crown is fitted, matched to your natural tooth.

CE-certified materials
We use titanium implants with the same certification standard required in the UK and across the European Union.
3D digital planning
Every implant is placed following a 3D scan of your mouth, for a precise and predictable result.
Zirconia crown included
The €400 price already includes the final zirconia crown, coloured and finished to blend with your natural teeth.
Written guarantee
Every implant is covered by a written international guarantee, with scheduled check-ups available remotely.

Your Journey

2 trips, one more tooth

Send Your X-ray
If you have one, send it via WhatsApp or the form. If not, we take one free of charge on arrival. We confirm your visit within 24 hours.
First Trip (2-3 days): Implant
Placement of the titanium implant under local anaesthetic. A temporary tooth is fitted if needed.

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know

Placement is done under local anaesthetic, so you shouldn't feel pain during the procedure. Mild swelling for a few days afterwards is normal and managed with standard pain relief.
With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, an implant typically lasts 20-25 years or more, often a lifetime. The crown may need replacing after 15-20 years.
In some cases a bone graft is needed before the implant. We assess this free of charge on your panoramic X-ray and let you know upfront, with the exact cost, before you book anything.
It depends on how many teeth are missing and where. For a few teeth, single implants work well; for a near-complete arch, All-on-4 or All-on-6 may be a better fit.
