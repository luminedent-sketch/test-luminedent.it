What Is All-on-6

How it actually works

Like All-on-4, but with 6 implants per arch instead of 4 — the recommended choice when available bone is thinner or you need a stronger base for chewing load.

6 implants per arch
Using 3D digital planning, a whole arch is supported by 6 implants, offering an extra anchor point compared with All-on-4 for cases that need it.
Fixed teeth the same day
At the end of surgery we fit a fixed temporary bridge — you never leave the clinic without teeth, not even for one night.
Final bridge after healing
After 3-6 months of osseointegration, the temporary bridge is replaced with your final zirconia bridge, custom-finished.
Chew like natural teeth
Unlike a removable denture, All-on-6 is fixed — no slipping, no adhesive, and you can eat what you like once healing is complete.
The Procedure

Digital precision, from day one

Every All-on-6 procedure is planned with 3D digital imaging, for precise and predictable implant placement.
