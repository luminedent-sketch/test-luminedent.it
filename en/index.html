Seven questions to ask any clinic abroad

Ask us too. Here are our answers, in writing, before you even ask.

Who carries out the treatment?
Dr. Bani, with 15 years of clinical experience, follows your case from review to check-ups. You can ask him questions before booking.
What materials do you use?
CE-certified implants and materials, the same mark required across the EU. For veneers we use E-max ceramic only, never composite.
Do I get the price in writing before booking flights?
Yes. You receive the quote and dates in writing before you spend anything on travel. If your case needs extra work, such as a bone graft, you know beforehand.
Is there a written guarantee?
Every treatment carries a written international guarantee that states exactly what happens if a problem appears.
What happens when I am back home?
Follow-ups at 3, 6 and 12 months, WhatsApp support for 12 months and, when needed, a remote check before we plan another trip.
What can raise the price?
Bone graft, sinus lift and extractions. We assess them from your X-ray and tell you before you book, never as a surprise.
How many trips will I need?
It depends: veneers and crowns usually one trip, implants and All-on-4/6 two, orthodontics two or more.

Frequently asked questions

How much do I save compared with the UK or Italy?
On average 65-75%, even after travel and accommodation. A single implant is €400 against typical UK private prices of £1,800-2,500; for All-on-4 the difference runs into thousands.
Is dental treatment in Albania safe?
It depends on the clinic, not the country. Check what you would check at home: CE-marked materials, clear communication in your language, a written guarantee and a full treatment plan before you book flights.
How do I pay?
In full, in euros, at the clinic when treatment takes place. Your exact price is confirmed in writing before you arrive.
How many times do I need to travel?
Single implant: usually two trips of 2-3 days. All-on-4/6: two trips of 4-5 days. Veneers: often one trip of 5-7 days.
What if I need help after I get home?
We first coordinate a remote follow-up and, if needed, arrange a new visit. We stay on WhatsApp for 12 months and every treatment carries a written guarantee.
Do you arrange transfers and hotel?
Yes. Airport-clinic transfers and your stay are organised by us and listed in your written quote.
