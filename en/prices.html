Frequently asked questions

Why is an implant €400 here and £1,800+ in the UK?
The materials are the same CE-certified standard. The difference is cost of living: rent, staff and running costs in Tirana are lower, and that shows in the price, not in the quality of the implant.
Is the €400 price final?
It covers a standard case: implant, zirconia crown, 3D diagnostics, surgery and checks. If your case needs extra work, such as a bone graft, we tell you before you book.
How do I get an exact price?
Send a recent X-ray or a few photos. The dentist reviews them and replies with your exact price.
