Dentures or a fixed bridge on implants: which to choose

Luminedent editorial team · updated 2 October 2026 · 4 min read

Dentures rest on the gums and can be taken out; a fixed bridge on implants is anchored to the bone and does not come out. At Luminedent the fixed All-on-4 solution costs €4,640 for the full mouth (UK £24,000+).

Who it suits

You need enough bone for implants (sometimes recoverable with a graft), healthy or treated gums and the ability to make two trips. Suitability is decided by the dentist after the X-ray.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a fixed bridge on implants in Albania?

€4,640 for the full mouth with All-on-4 (8 implants) or €9,120 with All-on-6 (12 implants). A single arch is on quote.

Can I switch from dentures to a fixed bridge?

Often yes: it depends on the available bone, assessed with the X-ray.

Do I need a bone graft?

Only if your X-ray requires it. Then we give you the exact cost in writing before you book.

Do you arrange transfers and hotels?

Yes, we arrange airport–clinic transfers and hotel stays. Costs are stated in the written quote.
